Sell-the-news was triggered by AI-capex-sustainability/valuation fears (AMD ~163x P/E), a strong Chinese open model (Moonshot Kimi K3), and a delayed Alphabet Gemini 3.5 Pro — narrative cracks, not a fundamental one *yet*. **Late-July hyperscaler capex (Jul 28–30) is now the arbiter of whether this is a valuation reset within the upcycle or the turn.**

## 3. KEY SIGNALS INTERPRETATION

- **Demand momentum: peaking — fundamentals still accelerating, the market has stopped believing it will get better.** Every fundamental read this period was *stronger*, not weaker: TSMC Q2 beat and raised, June accelerated to +67.9% YoY, ASML raised its full-year outlook. But the stocks fell into a bear market anyway. When the best possible earnings can no longer move the shares up, the marginal buyer is exhausted and momentum has peaked — regardless of what the trailing P&L says.
- **Supply tightness vs. oversupply: still tight *now*; the market began pricing 2027+ supply risk.** DRAM/HBM/CoWoS remain sold out and capex is being raised into that tightness (TSMC to $52–56B, ASML FY €43–45B). That is precisely the top-of-cycle capex surge that seeds the *next* oversupply — and TSMC's record capex guide was received as a *negative* (margin-compression / AI-cost-sustainability worry), the first time this cycle a capex raise was sold rather than bought.
- **Memory cycle direction: cresting (unchanged — no new data).** The controlling data is still TrendForce's 7/3 read: DRAM +13–18% / NAND +10–15% QoQ for Q3, decelerating from Q2's +63%/+75%, with a named consumer-affordability ceiling. Prices are still rising; the *pace* is rolling over. No fresh memory print this period — carried forward.
- **AI-driven demand strength vs. cyclical weakness: the AI *narrative* took its first real damage even as AI *demand* stayed strong.** The selloff's proximate triggers were AI-narrative cracks, not demand misses: (1) China's Moonshot **Kimi K3** open model landing "on par" with frontier US systems, pressuring the US-AI-leadership premium; (2) valuation air (AMD ~163x P/E) with no cushion; (3) Alphabet's **Gemini 3.5 Pro reportedly delayed**, denting the AI-timeline story. Underlying AI/HPC demand (TSMC HPC +20% QoQ, ASML "extremely strong") is intact — but the *valuation* built on that demand is what turned.

## 5. CYCLE POSITIONING CALL

- **Where we are: LATE upcycle — TURNING (escalated from "topping process underway" on 7/11).** The fundamental cycle (billings, sales, foundry revenue/margins, capex guides) is not just intact but *accelerating*. What changed decisively is the **leading market indicator**: the SOX/SPX ratio — historically the earliest read on the billings cycle — collapsed from a topping ~1.71 into a **bear market (~1.59, −20.2% from peak)** *on the best fundamental news the cycle can produce*, and underperformed a falling S&P doing it. Semi stocks peak while earnings are still rising; that is the definition of this juncture. The lagging aggregate data (record sales, record margins) confirms we are late, not early — and the market's refusal to reward it is the turn signal.
- **Confidence: MEDIUM.** Two-sided, and honestly so. Turn case: the highest-signal indicator did exactly the textbook topping thing, on cue, on blowout prints — that is hard to explain away. Not-yet-turned case: fundamentals are genuinely blowout and *raising*, Friday showed active dip-buying, the selloff's triggers were valuation/narrative (Kimi K3, Gemini delay, P/E) rather than a demand miss, and a $3.3T drawdown can be a valuation reset *within* an upcycle. The distinction between "expensive stocks corrected" and "the cycle turned" is not yet resolvable from price alone — hence medium.
- **Key risk that could invalidate this view:** *Upside (invalidates the turn)* — late-July hyperscaler earnings (Microsoft/Meta/Alphabet/Amazon, Jul 28–30) **raise** 2026 capex again with reassuring AI-demand commentary, semis V-bounce and reclaim leadership → this was a valuation reset, revert to plain LATE. *Downside (confirms the turn)* — any hyperscaler trims/defers capex or echoes an "excess-compute/digestion" line, or the capex-vs-operating-cash-flow crossover (tracked ~Q3 2026) starts to bite → "turning" becomes "turned," memory/consumer-price elasticity the first crack.
